Reliability Engineering

Availability is a number you choose, measure and spend. Everything else is opinion.

The discipline that separates SRE from operations is arithmetic. An SLO turns "it feels slow" into a measurable budget, and the budget decides when to ship features and when to stop. This course covers the definitions, the observability that makes them measurable, and the human process that keeps incidents from repeating.
